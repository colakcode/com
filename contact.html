What should I pay attention to before purchasing the “Get Premium” or “Remove Ads” products?
Before purchasing digital products such as “Get Premium” or “Remove Ads,” make sure you are signed in to the app with your email or Google account. This way, the purchase will be registered to your email account, and you will be able to use the app in its ad-free/premium version on any device where you sign in with that same email address.
I purchased one of the “Get Premium” / “Remove Ads” products, but I still see ads in the app. What should I do?
From time to time, after a purchase, digital products may not be applied correctly due to device performance, internet speed, or other factors while the app is running. If you encounter such a situation, please log out of the app and log back in. The product content will most likely be applied correctly.
I purchased a digital product and logged in and out of the app, but I still can’t access the product content. What should I do?
If you are unable to access the product content after your purchase, please contact me using the form above or via info@colakcode.com. Don’t forget to include the “Transaction ID” provided by Google Play during your purchase.
Will I experience any issues after purchasing a product?
If you experience any issues accessing your digital products after purchase, I will refund your payment once you email me your Google Transaction ID, and I will update your server record so you can use the app for free/premium. If you do not receive a quick response from me, you can also request a refund directly through Google Play. I apologize in advance for any inconvenience you may encounter.
