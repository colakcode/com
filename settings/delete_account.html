Terms for Deleting Your Data and Logging Out of Your Account!

If you delete your app account, your e-mail account, phone and operating system information and all graphic data you have created will be irretrievably deleted from our database and you will be logged out of your account. Your existing graphic data on your phone will not be affected and you will continue to use the application offline. If you purchase premium content, your premium content purchases will remain valid, but if you uninstall and reinstall the application, your ability to remove ads and holograms may be lost.

In practice, the ways you need to follow to delete your account and data.

In your application, go to the Settings section and click Delete Account. If you cannot see the Delete Account section, click the drop -down menu button on top right on the right Settings page and then enter the Delete Account. Carefully read the description in the Delete Account window. When you delete your account, you accept that your data will be deleted in a way that cannot be brought. Verify the approval box under the description. And with the delete button at the bottom, delete your account and data forever. In order to delete your data, you must log in with a mail account you have already registered. The account information and data you deleted belong to your account where you sign up -to -date. If you have multiple accounts and you want to delete the data of all your accounts, log in with all your accounts and repeat the transactions.


The data you will lose in general when you delete your account

- With your Google Account; email, name, language
- With your e-mail address; e-mail, password data is requested.
- Moreover ; phone make-model, device language, operating system version - The data you create according to the type of application; Graphics, 3D graphic data, graphic names and contents, spending and income records, etc.


Errors received during deleting your data

If you receive errors during deleting your data or observe that your account is not deleted. In the application, open the session, exit the application. Open it again and log in to your session and try to delete your account once again.

If you are still unable to delete your account, send an e-mail to the e-mail that you want to delete to the e-mail address below. Specify which application you want to delete which data completely or some of them. Your data will surely be deleted the way you want and you will return to you.
