About me

I'm an independent developer who enjoys building things across many fields — from Android apps and small libraries to simple games, electronics projects, and 3D designs. I’m not a professional studio or a big team; it’s just me, learning and improving step by step.


Over the years, I’ve created several Android applications, experimented with Unity game development, and modeled various parts in 3D using Blender and CAD tools. I like combining software and hardware whenever I can, and I treat each project as a chance to grow my skills.

My goal isn’t to be perfect, but to keep building useful, fun, and well-crafted things — and share them with anyone who might find them helpful.


My name is Kemal Çolak and please use my email address info@colakcode.com to contact me.
